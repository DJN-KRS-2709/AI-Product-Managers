The Juno Scenario

One scenario all six modules

You are the AI PM at RocketShip, a hyper-growth B2B SaaS platform for Enterprise Data Teams. Success has created a Signal Collapse — too many escalations, too many threads, too many open decisions. You've been given an innovation budget but no headcount.

So you ship Juno PM — an AI Associate PM embedded in Slack, Notion, and Jira. By M6 your Juno PM is a finalised repo (your portfolio).

Design Principles

Seven principles every module obeys

1. Build in every module

No empty modules. Every module ships one real artifact into your fork.

2. Individual-only

No group work. Self-review checklist + AI-review prompt + async share in #ai-pm-cohort.

3. One scenario

Juno, RocketShip, all six modules. No re-contexting overhead.

4. Frameworks as mental models

You leave with frameworks that work without your slides.

5. AI PM is different

We never let learners think their existing PM playbook is enough.

6. Effective tools

Bespoke interactive tools per module, with Copy-as-markdown / Export-to-repo.

7. AI as critique partner

Every exercise ends with an AI-review prompt — using AI to evaluate AI strategy.

Format

How it runs

  • Cadence: 6 sessions × ~2 hours.
  • Live: instructor walks the deck; learners build in the tools as they go.
  • Async: commit artifacts to your fork between modules; instructor responds in #ai-pm-cohort.
  • Submission: the URL of your finalised juno-pm/ fork, within 7 days of cohort end.
  • Grading: Application of Concepts · Credibility & Reasoning · Clarity · Strategic Thinking · 1/2/3 scale.
What you leave with

Portfolio + operating system

  • A public juno-pm/ repo demonstrating every M1–M6 framework.
  • A System Prompt + Lovable prototype.
  • An AI Strategy One-Pager + Decision Matrix.
  • An AI PRD with explicit RAG decisions.
  • An AI user flow with trust-gap mitigations.
  • An Agent Workflow Spec + control panel.
  • A three-layer eval stack + human rubric.
  • A finalised README — your PM Execution Plan + pitch.
