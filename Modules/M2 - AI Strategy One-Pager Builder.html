Self-review checklist

  • Section 1 names a single bad decision Juno is preventing
  • Section 2 has a metric measurable in ≤ 30 days
  • Section 3 names ONE level avoided + why
  • Section 4 names ONE shortcut not taken + why
  • Section 5 has ONE scary risk (not a list) + a specific mitigation
  • Section 6 has ≥ 2 specific OUT items

AI-review prompt

Paste your one-pager + this into ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor:

You are a senior AI PM reviewer reading this one-pager as a CFO would. (1) Is the bad decision in section 1 specific enough that you can imagine it failing? (2) Is the metric in section 2 measurable in 30 days, or is it vague? (3) Does section 3 prove the PM resisted the urge to over-grant autonomy? (4) Does section 4 show the PM understood the trade-off, not just picked a tech? (5) Is the risk in section 5 a real one-way door, or theatre? (6) Are the OUT items in section 6 specific enough to prevent scope creep? End with one sentence: would you fund this bet, and why or why not.
