Self-Review Checklist

  • Role names a specific persona and their expertise (years, domain)
  • Task starts with a verb and ties to RocketShip's actual bottleneck
  • At least 3 constraints, including a "do not" rule
  • Format describes columns, components, AND interaction (a button, a state)
  • Combined prompt is between 80 and 350 words

Artifact 1 · User Interview Transcript (Sarah, Data Analyst)
Interviewer: So, tell me about your workflow. User (Sarah, Data Analyst): honestly, it's been a week. My dog has been barking all morning, sorry if you hear him. Anyway, I log into RocketShip every Monday. The first thing I notice is that the new blue navigation bar is really bright, like hurts my eyes bright. Can we change that? But yeah, so I go to the 'Quarterly Reports' tab. I select my date range — usually last 90 days — and hit 'Generate PDF.' That works fine. But then — and this is the part that makes me want to scream — I try to click 'Export to CSV' because I need to pivot this in Excel. It spins for like 5 minutes and then just crashes. No error message. Just blank. I've lost hours because of this. I end up just taking screenshots of the table, which is stupid. Oh, and I'd love a dark mode.
What to notice. The dashboard will probably look great but the AI summary often misses the actual signal. The CSV export crash is the real Revenue Blocker. The blue nav bar is noise. The dark-mode request is filler. This is the “Beautiful Liar” problem — the prompt for Step 2 of the module fixes it.
