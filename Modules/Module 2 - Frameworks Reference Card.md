# Module 2 — Frameworks Reference Card

Quick-lookup card for M2.

---

## Fake-Good vs. Boring Killer (two-question filter)

1. Does it ladder to a real value frame?
2. Would it survive a 3x cost stress test?

Both yes → boring killer. Either no → fake-good.

---

## Four AI Value Propositions

- **Cost** — replace human labour.
- **Revenue** — new product surface, new willingness to pay.
- **Risk** — catch what humans miss.
- **Delight** — anticipate intent, reduce friction.

---

## The Three-Layer Model

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Bet + value frame + why now |
| **Mechanic** | What the product does |
| **Implementation** | Prompt / RAG / fine-tune / agent |

---

## Jobs × Risk × Autonomy

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| Job | What task is the AI doing? |
| Risk | Cost-of-wrong (reputation, regulation, revenue) |
| Autonomy | Suggest → Draft → Execute → Operate |

**Rule:** high-risk jobs cap at *Draft*.

---

## The Autonomy Levels

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Suggest | AI proposes; human decides |
| Draft | AI drafts v0.1; human edits + approves |
| Execute | AI acts; human reviews after |
| Operate | AI runs long horizons |

---

## AI Solution Decision Matrix (5 axes, score 1–5)

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| Value clarity | Does it ladder to a value frame? |
| Technical feasibility | Can current models do this well enough? |
| Data availability | Do you have / can you get the corpus? |
| Risk tolerance | Can you tolerate wrong outputs? |
| Strategic fit | Does it earn company oxygen vs. other priorities? |

**Verdict:** <3 average = don't ship. 4+ on all five = boring killer.

---

## Responsible AI — strategic guardrails (4 buckets)

- Compliance
- Safety
- Reliability
- Reputation

Each guardrail you commit to is a sales asset, not just a cost.

---

## AI Strategy One-Pager — 7 blocks

1. The bet (one sentence + value frame)
2. The user / job
3. The mechanic
4. The autonomy
5. The technical approach
6. The risk + guardrail
7. The success metric (measurable in 30 days)
