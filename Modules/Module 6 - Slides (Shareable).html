Measure AI Quality with Evals and Guardrails

95% accuracy is a 5% production disaster waiting to happen.

End state: Juno's eval stack + human rubric + finalised juno-pm/README.md.

Why Vibe Checks Fail

"It looks good in five prompts" is a trap

  • A handful of successful prompts is not a quality bar.
  • Production traffic exposes long-tail inputs.
  • Models drift; prompts decay; corpora go stale.
  • Without a harness, you can't tell whether a change improved things.
The 95% Accuracy Trap

The last 5% is where production risk lives

1,000 P0 thread triages/day × 5% wrong = 50 wrong outputs/day. Some silently wrong.

Eval design is the product surface that decides whether you can ship.

The AI Evals Stack

Three layers — always all three

LayerVolumeFidelityWhat it tells you
User feedbackHighestLowestWhat users do
Human evaluationMidHighWhat graders think
Automated evalsHighestMidWhat an LLM-judge or regression suite measures
Human Eval Rubric

Five components

  1. Dimensions (accuracy, tone, completeness, safety, citation correctness)
  2. Scale (1–5 or pass/fail) with anchor descriptions
  3. Examples — one per scale point
  4. Disagreement protocol
  5. Sampling cadence

Tool: M6 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html — spec dimensions, anchors, cadence; export.

PM's Role in Evaluations

Eval is product surface

  • PM owns the rubric and the golden set
  • PM defines the pass bar
  • PM decides the regression cadence
  • PM acts on eval data — model / data / architecture
