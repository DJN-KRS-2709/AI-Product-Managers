AI Product Management Certification

Tools Overview

Sixteen interactive tools, indexed by module. Each one is a single-file HTML app with localStorage persistence, "Copy as markdown" / "Download .md" actions, an in-tool self-review checklist, and an AI-review prompt. Outputs feed directly into your juno-pm/ fork.
