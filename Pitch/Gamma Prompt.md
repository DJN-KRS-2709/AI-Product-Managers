# Gamma Prompt — AI Product Management Certification Pitch Deck

Paste this into Gamma to regenerate a polished, presentation-ready version of the course's pitch deck. Source of truth is `AI Product Management - Pitch Deck.html`; this is the alternate "slide-style" rendering for stakeholders who expect a deck not a webpage.

---

## Use these settings in Gamma

- **Format:** Presentation (16:9)
- **Theme:** Dark, technical (e.g., "Obsidian", "Neon Tokyo", or any dark-monospace theme)
- **Density:** Dense but uncluttered — one visual idea per slide
- **Length:** 10 slides
- **Tone:** Confident, opinionated, slightly contrarian. Not corporate. Not marketing-soft.

---

## The prompt

> Create a 10-slide pitch deck for the **AI Product Management Certification** — a 6-module course that produces a public GitHub repo as the certification submission, instead of a slide deck. Use a dark, technical theme. Confident, opinionated tone.
>
> **Slide 1 — Title.** "AI Product Management Certification — The repo-as-portfolio AI-PM course." One subtitle line: "Six modules. One forkable project. Production-grade artifacts you ship to your own GitHub."
>
> **Slide 2 — The Problem.** "Most AI-PM courses leave you with notes, not artifacts." Bullets: You watch slides and take notes / You leave with vocabulary, not a portfolio / You can describe RAG, can't ship a RAG decision / You learn evals matter, don't leave with an eval stack.
>
> **Slide 3 — The Bet.** "This course is a repo, not a deck." Show six tiles: System Prompt · AI Strategy One-Pager · AI PRD · AI User Flow · Agent Workflow Spec · Eval Stack. Subtitle: "Every module pushes one real artifact into your `juno-pm/` fork."
>
> **Slide 4 — Course Arc.** A 6-row table: Module | Arc verb | Execution question. Use the verbs Bet, Decide, Specify, Trust, Orchestrate, Prove. Questions: What does an AI-native PM do differently? · Is this bet real, and can we ship it? · How do I write a spec engineers can build from? · How do I close the three trust gaps? · When do I let the system act on its own? · How do I prove this is shippable and stay shippable?
>
> **Slide 5 — The Scenario.** "One scenario the whole course: Juno PM at RocketShip." Two-paragraph framing: RocketShip is in a Signal Collapse. You build Juno PM, an AI Associate PM in Slack/Notion/Jira. Every framework lands against the same scenario. The artifact you build in M1 is the one you finalise in M6.
>
> **Slide 6 — What's Different.** Six tiles: Repo as portfolio · 16 bespoke interactive tools · Individual format (no groupwork) · AI as critique partner · Frameworks > content · Production-grade artifacts.
>
> **Slide 7 — Outcomes.** "What you can do Monday morning." 7 bullets — pressure-test an AI bet across 6 lenses · decide a RAG architecture against cost/speed/accuracy · write an AI PRD with corpus, eval plan, failure modes · architect a user flow with trust gaps closed · spec an agent in 9 sections · stand up a 3-layer eval stack · pitch the whole bet from one README.
>
> **Slide 8 — Audience.** Three personas as cards: The Crossover PM (traditional PM adding AI to existing surfaces) · The AI-Native PM (already shipping AI, needs a structured operating system) · The Founder / Staff PM (running the PM function for an AI bet).
>
> **Slide 9 — Format.** 6 sessions × ~2h, live · Async commits between sessions · Individual format (no breakouts, works in any cohort size including 1) · 7-day submission window post-cohort · Graded on 4 qualities, 1–3 scale.
>
> **Slide 10 — The Differentiator.** Hero text: "What you leave with: a public repo." Subtitle: "Not a certificate PDF. A real, hireable, peer-reviewable AI-PM portfolio. github.com/yourname/juno-pm." Call to action: "Fork the course repo. Run a cohort. Or run yourself through it."
>
> Keep typography crisp. Avoid stock-photo-style illustrations. Prefer monospaced or tech-leaning typography. Use the accent colour `#7c8cff` (soft indigo) for emphasis and `#22d3ee` (cyan) for tags/labels.
